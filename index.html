About

Operating at the intersection of infrastructure, capital, and intelligence.

Mike Ogbebor advises investors, institutions, and public-sector stakeholders on the strategic deployment of capital into infrastructure and real-asset projects across emerging markets.

He is the Founder of GoBeyond Advisory, a strategic investment and infrastructure advisory platform, and ECO Infrastructure, a digital settlement and FX rails initiative focused on cross-border liquidity corridors across Africa and emerging markets.

His work centers on long-term systems building — partnering with institutions, fintechs, and sovereign-adjacent programs to solve structural inefficiencies at scale.
